Install RDC

Install this independent gateway companion directly from your phone. No app store account is needed.

iPhone & iPad

Open this HTTPS page in Safari. Tap Share, choose Add to Home Screen, and tap Add. Open the RDC icon from your home screen.

Android

Open this HTTPS page in Chrome. Choose Install app or Add to Home screen in the browser menu. Open RDC from your launcher.

Connect this phone as a target

Installing the companion does not grant control over the phone's operating system. Native RDC automation requires an owner-authorized Appium or ADB host.

Connect an iPhone or iPad
  1. Use your Mac with full Xcode and the Appium XCUITest driver.
  2. Connect your phone, approve Trust and required Developer Mode settings, and provision WebDriverAgent for your device.
  3. Create an RDC iOS profile on that Mac with your exact device UDID and target app bundle ID.
  4. Use RDC's mobile list, start and observe tools after the worker loads the profile.
Connect an Android phone
  1. Install the Android SDK platform tools, a compatible JDK and Appium UiAutomator2 on your host.
  2. Enable USB debugging or explicitly pair wireless debugging. Approve the device prompt.
  3. Create an RDC Android profile using your exact serial, package and activity.
  4. Use RDC's mobile tools from your authorized AI client.

The phone companion is a read-only connection monitor. Device actions remain subject to the host's existing permissions and approvals.
